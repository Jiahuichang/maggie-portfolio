06 / FIRMWARE UPDATES

Improve the logic behind
a reliable update.

I raised the severity of firmware update failures using observed data, then brought app, middleware, and firmware teams together to examine the update logic. A failed controller update could affect the bike’s assistance, making recovery a product priority.

01

Map failures across the system

I reviewed update data and brought stakeholders together around the severity of the problem, mapping failures across devices, app versions, and firmware types.

02

Work through failure and recovery

I coordinated discussions on update steps, failure handling, and retry behavior with middleware and firmware teams. The visible UI changes focused on clearer troubleshooting after an update failed.

03

Coordinate the delivery

I documented the agreed behavior, coordinated delivery, and shared follow-up results several months after the improvements were released.

07 / APP RELIABILITY

Stabilize the foundation
riders depend on.

Android’s crash-free users rate was 78%. Crash complaints were a recurring theme in negative reviews, making stability a priority for the product.

01

Make failure patterns visible

I worked with the team to integrate Firebase Crashlytics and build a dashboard, prioritizing hotfixes by crash frequency and rider impact.

02

Test the difficult cases

I partnered with QA to expand edge-case testing, especially around BLE interactions, and set up staged releases to validate improvements progressively.
