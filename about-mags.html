ABOUT MAGGIE

Product thinking.
A designer’s perspective.

Hi, I’m Maggie, you can also call me Jiahui 嘉惠. I’m a Product Manager with a background in product design.

I turn ambiguous needs and complex workflows into clear product decisions, helping teams build experiences they can deliver, measure, and improve.

From design to product ownership.

My path from industrial design to Product Designer and then Product Manager has shaped how I work: I look closely at where people get stuck, then explore the systems, operational needs, and technical constraints behind the experience.

Across connected e-bike experiences and dealer service tools, I bring those perspectives together to help teams decide what matters, what to build first, and how to know whether it works.
