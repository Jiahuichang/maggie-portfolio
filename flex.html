HYENA FLEX · PRODUCT MANAGEMENT

From scattered vision
to a focused first release.

I turned an ambitious product vision into a shippable ride experience—rebuilding the roadmap around core rider value as development resources became limited.

Launched September 10, 2026 Explore the product decisions ↓
01 / OVERVIEW

A product built around
the everyday ride.

Flex helps riders record their rides, revisit their journeys, and see their progress. It brings ride tracking, challenges, rewards, and community into one experience.

My task was to turn those possibilities into a focused first release that the team could deliver, measure, and improve.

02 / CHALLENGE

More possibilities.
Less capacity to pursue them.

01

A scattered vision

Ride tracking, gamification, planting, community, rewards, and future e-bike features competed for attention.

02

An unclear reason to switch

Existing HRA riders did not immediately understand why they should move to Flex.

The question became: what must work in the first release for riders to experience real value?

05 / PRIORITIZATION FRAMEWORK

A release boundary,
not an endless feature list.

I weighed rider value, dependency risk, implementation effort, and whether a feature was necessary for the first usable experience. When capacity changed, I revisited the scope—not just the dates.

FIRST RELEASE

Protect the core experience

  • Ride recording, GPS maps & 3D ride records
  • Reports, weekly challenges & achievements
  • Rewards & planting
  • Feed, community, profile & notifications

DEFERRED / FUTURE SCOPE

Keep expansion off the critical path

  • Strava & HealthKit integrations
  • Expanded e-bike connectivity & status
  • Automatic ride start / finish & multi-bike support
  • Streaks, leaderboards, more languages & Android
  • Watch support & Live Activities

Scope summary, not a numerical scoring model. Deferred work is not presented as shipped functionality.

06 / KEY DECISIONS

The trade-offs that
made a release possible.

01

Core ride value before ecosystem expansion

Prioritized recording, ride visualization, and progress over additional integrations and advanced platform features.

02

Rebuild the roadmap around actual capacity

Re-scoped milestones and dependencies as resources decreased, giving the team a first-release plan it could execute.

03

Measure the journey beyond sign-up

Defined tracking around starting and completing rides so we could examine experienced value, not only account creation.

09 / POST-LAUNCH LEARNINGS

Rider behavior sharpened
the next set of priorities.

Value started with recording

Commuters cared about recording their everyday rides. Starting a ride emerged as a promising activation signal to examine alongside return behavior—not proof of causation.

Friction came before the first ride

Login and onboarding drop-off showed that some users left before experiencing the core product value.

Motivation needed to feel tangible

Planting reminders gave rewards a clearer purpose. Community usage also suggested we should not assume riders would behave like users of a standalone social network.

Early learnings guide hypotheses and priorities. Long-term retention impact is still being evaluated.

10 / ITERATIONS

From observations
to specific changes.

IMPLEMENTED

Make rewards easier to understand

Added EcoGem and planting reminders to connect progress with a tangible next action.

NEXT RELEASE

Bring riders closer to the first ride

Revised onboarding, earlier bike pairing, a clearer home hero, and contextual TipKit guidance.

ONGOING

Clarify the reason to move from HRA

Refine entry messaging so existing riders understand what Flex offers before they enter the flow.
